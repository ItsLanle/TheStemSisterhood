About The Event

The STEM Sisterhood is a digital space created to uplift, inform, and connect women navigating careers in science, technology, engineering, and math. This event and platform highlight personal stories, shared resources, and practical strategies for thriving in tech-related fields.

It is designed especially for women and girls interested in STEM, including students, aspiring professionals, and allies seeking community and support. Through reflection, storytelling, and engagement, The STEM Sisterhood fosters empowerment and representation in STEM spaces.
